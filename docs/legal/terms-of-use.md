## 1. What the app is — and isn't

Used Car Check is a **guided self-check**. It helps you look at a used car, ask the right questions and decide whether
to get it inspected. It is **not** a professional inspection, a mechanical diagnosis, a vehicle history report, a
valuation or legal or financial advice. A verdict such as "Looks good so far" only means you didn't find problems in
the checks you did. **Always get a pre-purchase inspection by an independent mechanic before you pay.**

## 2. Where the information comes from

- Recalls, owner complaints and crash ratings for US cars come from the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety
  Administration (NHTSA), as published. We are not affiliated with NHTSA or any manufacturer.
- Checklists, common problems by model, error-code explanations, symptom guides and repair-cost ranges are general
  guidance we prepared; they can be incomplete or wrong for a particular car. Repair costs are typical US ranges at
  independent shops; your price will vary.
- AI photo findings and text read from screenshots are generated by AI and can be wrong. They are suggestions: you
  decide whether to confirm them.
- Price checks only compare the listings you entered; asking prices aren't sale prices.

## 3. Safety

Only do the checks you can do safely. Never open a hot radiator cap, never get under a car that isn't safely
supported on level ground, and only test drive where it is safe and legal, with the seller's agreement. If a red
warning light comes on, stop and get the car checked.

## 4. Listings and sellers

Listing warning signs are common patterns, not proof that a seller did anything wrong. Don't use the app to accuse
anyone. Meet in a safe place and never pay before you've seen the car and its papers.
