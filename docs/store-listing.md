## Description (4000)

Buying a used car? Check it like a pro before you hand over the money.

Used Car Check is an honest second pair of eyes. We don't sell cars, loans or warranties, so we have no reason to
push you into a deal.

ADD THE CAR IN SECONDS
• Scan the VIN barcode on the door frame or windshield, take a photo of it, or type it
• No VIN yet? Pick the make and model; works for cars from any country

KNOW THE CAR BEFORE YOU GO
• Safety recalls, owner complaints by problem area and crash-test ratings from the U.S. National Highway Traffic
  Safety Administration (NHTSA), for US cars
• Common problems for the model and engine, and what to check for each one

A GUIDED 15-MINUTE INSPECTION
• A step-by-step checklist that puts this model's known problem areas first
• Take photos of tires, rust, paint and the dashboard: AI points out what it sees, you confirm or dismiss
• Works offline, except AI photo checks

A CLEAR VERDICT
• Looks good so far · Negotiate · Get a mechanic to check it first · Walk away
• Likely repair costs as a range, the exact questions to ask the seller, and a friendly or firm negotiation script

SPOT SCAMS AND BAD PRICES
• Paste the listing, or add screenshots, to catch common warning signs like wire-transfer requests or title problems
• Compare the price with similar listings you found

ALSO
• Diagnose: look up a check-engine code or describe what the car is doing
• Garage: keep every car you're considering and compare them side by side
• Short guides: tire date codes, the coin tread test, test-drive routes, paperwork

PREMIUM
Your first full inspection is free, and vehicle reports are always free. For more, choose:
• 30-day Car Hunt Pass: one payment, no subscription. It simply ends after 30 days.
• Pro, yearly: for people who check many cars. Renews yearly; cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.

IMPORTANT
Used Car Check is a guided self-check, not a professional inspection or vehicle history report. Always get a
pre-purchase inspection by an independent mechanic before you pay. Photo findings are generated by AI and can be
wrong. Recall, complaint and rating data: U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA). Not affiliated
with NHTSA or any car manufacturer.

## Review notes (App Review Information → Notes)

No account or login is needed. To try it: open the app, tap **Check** → **Type the VIN** →
`1N4AL3AP4DC900001` (a 2013 Nissan Altima) → Look it up → Save. The report shows NHTSA recalls and complaints.
Tap **Start the inspection** to see the guided checklist; answer a few items and open **See the verdict**.
AI photo checks need a photo of a real car part (tire, dashboard). Purchases: the 30-day pass is a non-renewing
in-app purchase; the yearly plan is an auto-renewable subscription. Restore is on the paywall and in Settings.
The app does not give mechanical, legal or financial advice: every verdict says to get a professional
pre-purchase inspection.
